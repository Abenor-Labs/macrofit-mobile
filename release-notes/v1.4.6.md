## Meal photos
- **Fixed: meal photos failed every time.** The photo model we used was retired by its provider; photos now go to a newer one.
- **Add a note to a photo.** After you take or pick a photo, it waits above the message box. Type how much you ate or what's in it ("ate half", "this is ragi mudde"), then tap Send. The note counts more than the photo, so the portions come out right. Tap ✕ to remove the photo, or Send to go without a note.
- The Photo button on the widgets works the same way.

## Fixes
- An error from the Coach no longer squeezes into a narrow column.
- A photo that fails now says so in plain words, with Retry, instead of showing a code.


## New here? How to install
1. On your Android phone, download **MacroFit-v1.4.6.apk** from the Assets list below.
2. Open the file. If Android asks, tap **Settings → Allow from this source**, then go back and tap **Install**.
3. If Google Play Protect shows a warning, tap **More details → Install anyway**. The app isn't on the Play Store yet, so Play Protect doesn't know it.

After that, the app updates itself from here: Profile → App version.
