New: one widget for food and training. Your weight now syncs with Google Fit both ways.

## New widget: MacroFit
One card on your home screen that follows your day. Add it from **Profile → Home screen widgets → MacroFit**.
- **Food side:** calories left and your macros. For two hours after a workout it shows the protein you still need.
- **Training side:** your next session with last time's weights, or the set you're on during a workout.
- It shows training while you're working out, and from 3 pm on a training day. The rest of the day it shows food.
- **Train** switches to training and **Food** switches back. **Water** adds 250 ml without opening the app.

The Today and Training widgets are still there. We'd like to know which one you keep, so tell us what you think.

## Health Connect and Google Fit
- **Weight syncs both ways on its own.** Weigh-ins from Google Fit now show up in MacroFit when you open it, and weights you log here (including through the Coach) go to Health Connect.
- Deleting a meal or a workout, or setting water back to zero, now removes it from Health Connect too.
- Meals logged for an earlier day now land on that day, not today.
- Fixed: after allowing access to past data, MacroFit still said only the last 30 days could be imported.

Google Fit only shows MacroFit data if Fit's **Sync Fit with Health Connect** setting is on.

## Other fixes
- If the Coach loses its connection, it now tries again on its own once before showing an error.
- A photo that fails can be retried without taking it again.
- Packaged-food search now runs when you press search, so it no longer comes back empty.
- Help now explains where the food numbers come from, and links to the privacy policy.


## New here? How to install
1. On your Android phone, download **MacroFit-v1.4.5.apk** from the Assets list below.
2. Open the file. If Android asks, tap **Settings → Allow from this source**, then go back and tap **Install**.
3. If Google Play Protect shows a warning, tap **More details → Install anyway**. The app isn't on the Play Store yet, so Play Protect doesn't know it.

After that, the app updates itself from here: Profile → App version.
