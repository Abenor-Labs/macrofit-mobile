# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 2 October 2026
**Contact:** mahamadsuhail9666@gmail.com

This policy covers the MacroFit Android app ("the app", "we"). It explains what the app stores, where it goes, and how to get it deleted. The short version: your data is used to run the app for you, and nothing else. There are no ads, no analytics or tracking tools, and we never sell your data.

## 1. Using the app without an account

You can use the app as a guest. In guest mode everything you log stays on your phone. Nothing is sent to our servers, except the food searches and update checks described in sections 5 and 6.

The AI coach and meal photo analysis need an account, because they run on our server.

## 2. What we store when you create an account

**Account details:** your email address and a password. The password is stored hashed by our authentication provider. We never see it.

**Your app data,** synced so it survives a new phone and works on the web app:

- Profile: age, sex, height, weight, activity level, goal and targets
- Food diary, custom foods and saved meals
- Weigh-ins and body measurements (for example waist and hips)
- Workouts, training programs, custom lifts and personal records
- Fasting sessions and streaks
- Progress photos, if you add any

This data is stored with **Supabase**, our database provider, in its Tokyo, Japan (ap-northeast-1) region. Access rules on the database let each account read and write only its own data. Progress photos are kept in private storage that only your account can open.

**Usage counters:** for each account we count how many AI requests were made per minute and per day, so we can enforce fair-use limits. Counters older than two days are deleted.

## 3. The AI coach and meal photos

When you chat with the coach, the app sends the following to our server:

- your message
- a summary of what the coach needs to answer well: recent meals, targets, weight trend, water, steps, training, and anything you asked it to remember

Our server is hosted on **Vercel**. It passes this to our AI provider, **Nebius AI Studio**, which generates the reply.

When you analyse a meal photo, the photo is sent the same way so the dishes can be recognised. We do not keep the photo on our server after the analysis.

Your chat history and the coach's memories are stored on your phone, not on our server.

AI replies and photo estimates can be wrong. The app is not a medical device and does not give medical advice. Talk to a doctor or dietitian before major changes to your diet or training.

## 4. Health Connect

If you choose to connect Android Health Connect, the app may:

- **Read:** steps, weight, height, body fat, and calories burned (total and active)
- **Write:** weight, body fat, nutrition, hydration, and exercise sessions

We use Health Connect data only to show it in the app and to make your targets and coach advice more accurate. We do not use it for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not share it with anyone except as needed to provide those features (as described in sections 2 and 3). Our use of information received from Health Connect follows the Health Connect Permissions policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

You can turn off any permission at any time in Health Connect settings.

## 5. Food search

When you search for a food, the search text is sent to public food databases:

- **USDA FoodData Central** (United States Department of Agriculture)
- **Open Food Facts**, only when you search packaged foods

These services see the search text and your phone's IP address, as with any website. No account details are sent.

## 6. App updates

The app checks GitHub for new versions and downloads updates from GitHub Releases. GitHub sees your IP address when this happens.

## 7. Notifications

Reminders (meals, water, workouts, weigh-ins) are scheduled on your phone. They do not go through our servers.

## 8. How long we keep data, and deleting it

We keep your account data until you delete it.

To delete your account and all synced data, including progress photos and usage counters, email **mahamadsuhail9666@gmail.com** from the address you signed up with. We will delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

Data stored only on your phone is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

## 9. Your rights

You can ask us to:

- show you the data we hold about you
- correct it
- delete it
- answer a question or complaint about how we handle it

Email **mahamadsuhail9666@gmail.com**. This contact also serves as our grievance officer contact under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

## 10. Children

The app is meant for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

## 11. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Database access rules keep each account's data separate. No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a breach that affects your data, we will tell you.

## 12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will also say so in the app.
