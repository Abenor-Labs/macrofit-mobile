## What is worth protecting

The action echo. The model says what it logged, the app renders what actually landed with real
macro chips, and `message.discarded` reports items the client refused even though the model
claimed them. Most chat integrations trust the model's own summary, which lets a silent drop
become a wrong diary. This is the reason the top of the screen reads as trustworthy while the
reply below it did not.

## The two P0s, and why they were the same bug

Both were the app failing to do its half of the job and the model being blamed for the result.

**Guessed macros.** The reply said "approx 13.5g from curry + 2g from buttermilk ≈ 48g total
protein so far" while the exact figure sat in the store and was rendered on the same screen.
Three layers were complicit: `ChatContextEntry` had no macro fields, the client mapped
`e.food` down to `{id, name, meal, calories}`, and the prompt sent only `todayCalories`. The
model estimated because estimating was the only move it had.

**Markdown as source.** No markdown dependency exists in the project and the bubble rendered
through a plain `<Body>`, so `###`, `**bold**` and pipe tables arrived as literal characters.
The fix is deliberately not a parser: a three-column table is unreadable at roughly 300 points
whether parsed or not. The prompt forbids tables and headings; `AssistantText` renders bold and
bullets and strips the rest, falling through to plain text on anything unrecognised.

## Still open

- **No streaming.** A long reply is a spinner then a wall. The largest perceived-speed win
  available on this screen.
- **Reply length is a soft constraint.** Prompt rules ask for six lines; models drift. A client
  cap with "show more" would make it structural.
- **Action cards are read-only.** They carry an entry ID and offer no undo.
- **Emoji density**, against an app whose visual language is restrained jade and Fraunces.
- **The detector cannot measure this file.** It parses markup; this is React Native inline
  styles. Not a clean result, an absent one.

## Deployment note
